I am a Ph.D. candidate at Korea University, advised by Seungryong Kim and Paul Hongsuck Seo, and a research intern at Adobe. My research focuses on general perception in video — especially Segment Anything and Matting Anything (VideoMaMa, VideoMaMa++) — and its application to video understanding.

I have interned at Adobe in San Jose, where I was fortunate to work with Seoung Wug Oh and Joon-Young Lee. Earlier, I was a research intern at NAVER Search U.S. working with Dongchan Kim, and a Language AI researcher at the NLP Center of NCSOFT, working with Ayoung Moon and Seungjin Lee.
